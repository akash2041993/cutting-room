Cutting Room

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Cutting Room collects only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data and we do not send marketing.

What we collect

  • Account: your email, your name, your phone number, and whether you joined as an Editor or a Client. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider. We cannot read it.
  • Editor profile: headline, city, about text, skills, tools, price, portfolio link and contact details you choose to add.
  • Client jobs: title, type, brief, budget, deadline, length and tools.
  • Applications and messages: the note and quote an Editor sends on a job, and the status the Client sets.
  • Notifications: the "under review / approved / rejected / paid" messages shown inside the app.
  • Payment: Razorpay takes your payment. We receive the payment ID, amount, time and your email. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and show your post to the people who are allowed to see it.
  • To review posts before they go live (the owner reads every submitted post, with your name, email and phone, so we can check it is real and contact you about the result).
  • To take the one-time fee and unlock your account.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, answer support requests, and meet legal and tax duties.

Who can see what

  • An approved Editor profile (including price, portfolio and contact) is visible to paid Clients. An approved job is visible to paid Editors.
  • An Editor's application is visible to the Client who owns that job.
  • Unapproved or rejected posts, your phone number and your email are not shown to other members.
  • We run the app; the owner can see all posts and member details for review and support.

Companies that help us run the app

  • Supabase stores the database and handles login. Servers may be outside India.
  • Razorpay processes payments.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app download.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the app opens.

We share your data with them only so the app can work, or when the law requires.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your login, profile, posts, applications and notifications are deleted. We keep payment records (payment ID, amount, date, email) for as long as accounting and tax law require.

Your choices

  • Edit your details and posts inside the app, and delete your account there at any time.
  • To see, correct or remove anything you cannot change yourself, write to .
  • The app is not for anyone under 18.

Security

Data is stored with access rules so that members can only read what they are allowed to read, and connections are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you do not use elsewhere.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy in an important way we will tell you in the app. Questions or complaints: . We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 30 days.
