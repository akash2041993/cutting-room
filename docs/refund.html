Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

You pay Cutting Room a one-time fee of ₹500 after we approve your post. This page says when that fee is refunded.

You get a full refund if

  • You were charged twice (or more) for the same account. The extra payments are refunded.
  • Money left your account but your account was not unlocked, and we could not fix it within 3 working days.
  • We removed or could not publish your approved post by our own mistake, within 7 days of your payment.

No refund if

  • You changed your mind after your post went live.
  • You did not get messages, jobs or hires. The fee is for using the platform; we do not promise results.
  • Your post or account was rejected, removed or closed because it broke our Terms.

How to ask

Email from the address you registered with and include the Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_ and is in your Razorpay receipt email or SMS). We reply within 48 hours. Approved refunds go back to the same card, UPI or bank account through Razorpay and usually reach you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Payment disputes

Please contact us before you raise a dispute with your bank. We will sort it out quickly.
